# MedQuAD Retrieval Benchmark — Lexical vs Semantic

_Generated by `python -m benchmarks.medquad.run_benchmark` on 2026-06-24._

Real medical questions from [MedQuAD](https://github.com/abachaa/MedQuAD)
(NIH / NCI / CDC sources). Each query must retrieve its own answer passage from a
corpus of **1500 real passages** (1500 held-out queries).
These are real numbers — well below 100% — and the point is the **gap**.

| Metric | BM25 (lexical) | MiniLM (semantic) | Δ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recall@1 | 0.657 | **0.750** | +9.3 pts |
| Recall@5 | 0.864 | **0.881** | +1.7 pts |
| Recall@10 | 0.880 | **0.900** | +2.0 pts |
| MRR@10 | 0.747 | **0.808** | +6.1 pts |

Embedding model: `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2`. Both retrievers share the same tokenizer/stemmer
for the lexical side; semantic encodes on CPU
(index+score: 64.2s vs lexical 0.5s).

## Where semantic wins

Real questions the keyword retriever ranked poorly (or missed) but the embedding
retriever put near the top — usually because the question and answer use
different words for the same concept:

| Question | Topic | BM25 rank | Semantic rank |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| _What are the symptoms of WaterhouseFriderichsen syndrome ?_ | WaterhouseFriderichsen syndrome | not found | 1 |
| _What is (are) Diabetes ?_ | Diabetes | not found | 2 |
| _What are the treatments for Prescription and Illicit Drug Abuse ?_ | Prescription and Illicit Drug Abuse | not found | 2 |
| _What to do for What I need to know about Gestational Diabetes ?_ | What I need to know about Gestational Diabetes | not found | 1 |
| _What is (are) Anxiety Disorders ?_ | Anxiety Disorders | not found | 1 |

> **Takeaway:** on a real, messy corpus the lexical retriever is a solid
> baseline, but semantic retrieval meaningfully improves recall — exactly the
> limitation the fictional-clinic eval flagged, now measured on real data.
