# Moonleaf Sleep Clinic Referral

The Moonleaf Sleep Clinic is a fictional specialty service for sleep disruption
in the Northstar Fictional Clinic training set. Referral is considered when a
patient reports repeated dream-cycle interruption, daytime fog, or moonleaf tea
exposure in the invented setting.

The patient pathway explains that referral means a clinician wants a closer look
at sleep patterns. It should not imply a serious diagnosis. Patients are asked
to bring a seven-night sleep diary and a list of evening routines.

Doctor-facing notes say to include sleep diary status, medication-like fictional
substances, shift schedule, and safety-sensitive work status. Urgent escalation
is used only for severe confusion, collapse, or immediate safety risk.
