# Luma Cough Syndrome

Luma Cough Syndrome is a fictional respiratory irritation pattern described in
the Northstar Fictional Clinic training set. It is associated with a dry,
glowing cough after exposure to blue lantern dust in the clinic's invented
world.

The fictional care pathway says that most mild cases are handled with hydration,
rest from lantern-dust exposure, and a follow-up message after two fictional
days. The patient-facing guide avoids diagnosis language and encourages a
clinician review if symptoms change.

Protocol escalation triggers include shortness of breath, chest tightness,
confusion, persistent high fever, or symptoms lasting more than five fictional
days. Doctors are instructed to check exposure timing, rule out Amber Fever in
the fictional differential, and document the source of lantern-dust contact.
