# Amber Fever Follow-Up

Amber Fever is a fictional febrile syndrome in the Northstar Fictional Clinic
training set. It is not a real illness. The clinic guide describes warm amber
skin shimmer, fatigue, and a temperature log pattern after travel through the
invented Glass Orchard district.

The follow-up pathway asks patients to keep a twice-daily symptom note and
schedule a fictional nurse check-in within three days. Patient communication
must say that the guide is educational and cannot confirm a diagnosis.

Doctor-facing protocol points include reviewing exposure history, confirming
temperature trend, checking whether Luma Cough symptoms are also present, and
using the clinic's fictional escalation checklist if fever persists beyond four
days.
