# Glowstone Allergy Visit

Glowstone Allergy is a fictional contact sensitivity in the Northstar Fictional
Clinic training set. It may appear in the invented world after handling
decorative glowstone powder and is described with itchy hands, silver speckles,
or mild rash.

The patient guide says to stop handling the fictional powder, wash exposed skin,
and contact the clinic if swelling, breathing trouble, or spreading rash occurs.
It avoids telling the patient they definitely have an allergy.

Doctor protocol asks for exposure route, rash distribution, prior reactions,
and whether respiratory symptoms are present. Escalation is required for airway
symptoms, facial swelling, or rapidly progressive rash in the fictional protocol.
